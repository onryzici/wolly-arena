# Free Firearm Sound Library — CC0

Created and recorded by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney.
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
License: CC0 1.0. Six source WAV entries were extracted from the Prepared SFX Library and each verified against its archive CRC32. The unused remainder of the large archive was not retained as a complete download. Selected recordings: 1917 revolver, AR-15 rifle, Charles Daly shotgun, two per family. Edited into short mono cues, normalized to 0.78 peak, with end fades; source hashes and exact trimming times are recorded in Assets/Woolly/ThirdParty/FreeFirearm/selected-assets.json.
